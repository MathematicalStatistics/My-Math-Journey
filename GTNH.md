My GTNH Journey:

GTNH is a modpack that I keep coming back to eventually.

The summer I graduated high school was the furthest I made it in GTNH. I reached the [extreme voltage (EV)](https://wiki.gtnewhorizons.com/wiki/Extreme_Voltage_(EV)) 
and made a [tier 2 rocket](https://wiki.gtnewhorizons.com/wiki/Rockets) to go to Mars. 
I had around 350 hours into that world (I don't have the save file anymore, oh well). I burned out because I didn't think of infrastructure back then.

GTNH is a modpack many people keep attempting because your run isn't optimized the first time you play it. 
I now see how important infrastructure is via batch-crafting and hoarding materials.
I'm currently playing it right now on the beta 2.9 v2 which can be downloaded on their Discord server. 
This time, I think journaling will help me reach the ultimate goal of the [stargate.](https://wiki.gtnewhorizons.com/wiki/Stargate)
