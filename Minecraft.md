[Minecraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft) is a [sandbox game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandbox_game) 
where you can choose any adventure you'd like. Think of it like 3D simulation Legos.

Minecraft is my favorite game of all time. I think I have around 8k hours of playtime on it throughout my life. I've been playing it since the summer of 3rd grade.

I first found out about modded (additional 3-rd party content) Minecraft through the [Technic Launcher](https://www.technicpack.net/download) about a year after getting Minecraft. 
[Tekkit](https://www.technicpack.net/modpack/tekkitmain.552547) was one of the first modpacks I played (modpacks are a collection of mods put together). 
[Project E](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/projecte) has EMC values for every item which you can kind of think of it as some sort of currency. 
There's another mod called [Industrial Craft 2 (IC2)](https://wiki.industrial-craft.net/index.php?title=Main_Page) which adds many new machines (kind of like working in a factory). 
[Factorio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factorio) is another game that was actually inspired by modded Minecraft. [Gregtech](https://gregtech.overminddl1.com/) is an addon for 
IC2 that is made by GregoriousT.

I did make a passion project for Minecraft when I was in 6th grade but it is gone.

In 8th grade, I discovered a modpack called [Gregtech: New Horizons (GTNH).](https://www.gtnewhorizons.com/) It is supposed to be the most complex modpack 
that exists for Minecraft. I've attempted it many times but didn't get
really far into the game. Gregtech is a mod that expands IC2 even further by adding more [voltage tiers](https://wiki.gtnewhorizons.com/wiki/Main_Page). 
Base IC2 only has 5 voltage tiers while GTNH has 13. Every recipe in the game has 
Gregtech intertwined to it. [There's a lot of number crunching and data analysis](https://wiki.gtnewhorizons.com/wiki/Quick_Links)
behind this game such as energy consumption in EU/t for electricity. Applied Energistics 2 (AE2) is also in this 
modpack which is like a computer simulator for auto-crafting complex recipes. It does take some time for the recipes but there are ways to speed them up such as upgrading machines. It also 
taught me the basics of chemical engineering (petrochem) [also organic chemistry] and electrical engineering circuit building.
